# Changelog

Notes shown on the dash's "What's New" screen after an update come from here.
Add a `## vX.Y.Z - Short title` section matching the release's git tag, with
`-` bullet points. The dash reads only the version from the heading.

## v1.1.1 - SpooledOS Community
- Live readings from your DS1 over WiFi: boost as a wide bar with its target marked and the last ten seconds behind it, and six tiles a page for lambda, fuel pressures, wastegate duty, knock and more
- Hold any tile to move it or change what it shows, from the dash's own values or any channel your DS1 streams
- Page 2 shows G-force as a square plot with lateral, accel/brake and peak numbers, or any channel you choose
- Readings change colour only when they cross a warning threshold
- Switch between your DS1's maps from the screen, with a confirm first, and give them your own names under Settings → Map names
- Logs every drive and every pull in the DS1's own log format, so they open in the tools tuners already use
- Download logs to your phone over the dash's WiFi at spooled.local/logs, or scan the QR code in Settings
- Log graphs on the dash itself
- Fault codes and the check-engine status, with descriptions read from your own DS1
- Ethanol blend calculator for E85 and pump gas
- Brightness, °F/°C, time zone, and joining your home WiFi from Settings
- Updates over WiFi from Settings, with what's new shown before you install
